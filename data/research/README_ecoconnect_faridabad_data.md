# EcoConnect — Faridabad e-waste research candidates

Prepared: 2026-10-09

## Purpose
Internal research/QA file for Member 3 (Data, QA & Demo Lead). It contains candidate records transcribed from an indexed extract of an official HSPCB PDF. It is **not a production-ready verified provider list**.

- Indexed HSPCB PDF, "List of E-waste Recyclers / Dismantler": https://hspcb.gov.in/uploads/pages/ewaster_66ac829eb3965.pdf
- HSPCB Faridabad District Environment Plan: https://hspcb.gov.in/uploads/pages/faridabad_669bdc88dbbb3.pdf

## Important limitations
- The PDF is a historical record; current registration/operating status was not established from this extract.
- Source publication date and reuse license were not established. `source_license=not_checked` and `listing_permission=pending_clarification` are intentional.
- Names and addresses are transcribed from the search-index extract and should be checked against the original PDF before use.
- Do not publish these rows as currently authorized providers. All rows have `active=false` and `review_status=pending`.
- No phone numbers, coordinates, opening hours, accepted items, or pickup/drop-off services were invented. Those fields remain blank until verified.
- These are e-waste recycler/dismantler candidates, not necessarily household drop-off points.
